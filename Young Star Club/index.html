Young Star Club

Kargeswar Roy Road, Ward No. 7, Sapatgram, Assam

Young Star Club has united the youth and families of Sapatgram since 1985 through traditional Pujas, national celebrations, competitive sports, and grassroots community service.

Heritage & Devotion

Annual Puja & Cultural Celebrations

Traditional celebrations bringing together families and neighbors of Ward No. 7 and Sapatgram.

Annual Grand Celebration

Shree Shree Kali Puja

Our flagship annual celebration featuring traditional Shyama Puja rituals, artistically crafted pandals, dazzling lighting across Ward No. 7, community Bhog distribution, and cultural nights for all residents.

Youth & Education

Shree Shree Saraswati Puja

Dedicated to students and youth, celebrated with morning Pushpanjali, traditional attire, educational and cultural competitions, and festive community gatherings in the heart of Sapatgram.

Patriotism & Unity

National Days & Patriotic Observances

Honoring the nation and our freedom fighters with flag hoisting and community gatherings.

23rd January

Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Jayanti

Observing Parakram Diwas with floral tributes to Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose, patriotic speeches, and youth inspiration programs organized by club members.

26th January

Republic Day Celebration

Ceremonial hoisting of the National Flag at the club premises, followed by the National Anthem, sweets distribution for local children, and friendly neighborhood matches.

15th August

Independence Day Celebration

Celebrating India's freedom with flag hoisting, homage to freedom fighters, community march, and day-long sports and cultural events for Ward No. 7 residents.

Fitness & Team Spirit

Sports & Tournaments

Encouraging physical fitness and healthy competition through outdoor and indoor games.

01

Cricket

Annual knockout tennis-ball cricket tournaments and weekend friendly matches encouraging local sporting talent.

02

Badminton

Evening floodlight badminton tournaments during the winter season with singles and doubles categories.

03

Carrom & Indoor

Regular club-house carrom and indoor board games bringing all age groups together every evening.

Community Welfare

Social Activities & Public Service

Beyond festivals and sports, Young Star Club actively stands for social responsibility.

Clean & Green

Cleanliness & Plantation Drives

Regular cleanliness drives around Kargeswar Roy Road, Ward No. 7, and tree plantation initiatives for a greener Sapatgram.

Public Support

Community Relief & Assistance

Extending helping hands during local emergencies, medical support, and essential distribution for underprivileged families.

Social Awareness

Health & Youth Awareness

Organizing blood donation camps, health check-ups, and anti-drug awareness initiatives for local youth.
